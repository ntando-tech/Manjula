INTRODUCTION

Manjula Group (PTY) Ltd was established in 2018 by Ndumiso Ncube who is an esteemed executive board member at the Ilembe Chamber of Commerce, Industry and Tourism.Manjula Group (PTY) Ltd is a dynamic logistics company specializing in the transportation of goods across KwaZulu-Natal and South Africa. In its early inception, Manjula Group (PTY) Ltd proudly launched with a valuable asset, a 12-ton truck contracted to Ambro Distribution. It was operated by a committed team of three professionals, comprising of a highly skilled driver and two efficient assistants. Our operational reach extended across key regions, including KwaZulu-Natal, Gauteng, Mpumalanga, Limpopo, and the Eastern Cape, establishing us as a trusted and reliable partner within these regions. Our commitment to excellence is reflected in the professional relationships we cultivate with valued clients, such as CMA CGM CCIS Durban, Baracuda Logistics, and esteemed stakeholders like Transnet, the Department of Transport, the Department of Labour, and SAPS.
