ABOUT US

Manjula Group (PTY) Ltd is a prominent logistics company with a strong presence in KwaZulu-Natal and across South Africa. Our core expertise lies in the efficient transportation of goods. We maintain a strategic partnership with the National Interaction Center and Transnet Port Terminal Updates, allowing us to stay updated every two hours. This invaluable collaboration enables us to seamlessly schedule appointments for the pickup and delivery of both import and export shipments, including 20-foot and 40-foot containers. With a commitment to reliability and precision, we have become a trusted name in the logistics industry.

Why choose Us

At Manjula Group (PTY) Ltd, we take immense pride in our dedication to forging enduring and strong partnerships with a diverse range of esteemed clients, including, but not limited to, industry leaders like CMA CGM CCIS Durban, and Baracuda Logistics. Our commitment extends beyond just client relationships; we place a high value on collaborating with essential stakeholders such as Transnet, the Department of Transport, the Department of Labour, and the South African Police Service (SAPS). Our team is at the heart of what makes us exceptional. Comprising dedicated and skilled professionals, we are fully committed to the secure and punctual delivery of your cargo. Through effective communication and proactive engagement, we prioritize the cultivation of these mutually beneficial relationships, ensuring seamless operations and sustainable growth for all parties involved. Our record of accomplishment, reliability and excellence speaks to our ability to deliver on our promises and makes us the preferred choice for all your logistics needs.
